# Maddox Chain — Artist Bio

Maddox Chain writes from the dirt side of the map — where the bar still has a jukebox-shaped hole in the wall, the truck still knows the long way home, and the quiet after goodbye is louder than the fight that caused it.

Raised on red dirt, broken promises, and late-night radio, Chain’s sound sits between outlaw country and dark country grit: raspy male vocals, heavy twang, and a pulse that hits like clubland after midnight. The songs don’t polish the truth. They pour whiskey on it, light it, and let it ride shotgun.

His catalog moves through working-man pride, small-town Saturday nights, empty chairs at Sunday dinner, wanted posters, and the kind of love that leaves the porch light on anyway. One minute it’s tailgates and bad ideas. The next it’s smoke over the backroads and one foot already in the grave.

Maddox Chain isn’t here to make country prettier. He’s here to make it honest — mean when it needs to be, proud when it earns it, and dark enough to feel true.

## Short bio

Maddox Chain is dark country with a pulse. Outlaw grit, red-dirt storytelling, and late-night heat — built for the ones still running from the same damn town.
